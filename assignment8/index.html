Assignment 8: Background Styling Attributes

1. background-color: steelblue
Sets a solid color as background. No image needed.
2. background-image (default repeat)
Small tile image repeats to fill entire area.
3. background-repeat: no-repeat
Image appears only once. Gray fills the rest.
4. background-repeat: repeat-x
Image repeats horizontally only (one row along the top).
5. background-repeat: repeat-y
Image repeats vertically only (one column on the left).
6. background-position: center center
Image is placed exactly at the center of the box.
7. background-size: cover
Image scales to fully cover the box. No gaps, may crop.
8. background-size: contain
Image fits fully inside the box. Gray fills gaps.
9. background-attachment: fixed
Background stays fixed when page is scrolled down.
10. All combined
image at right-bottom, 40% size, no-repeat, color fills rest.
