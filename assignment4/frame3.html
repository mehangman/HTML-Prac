Topic 3: Precautions to be taken in Covid 19

To prevent infection and to slow transmission of COVID-19, it is essential to follow public health guidelines. Simple precautions can make a huge difference in protecting yourself and others.

Key Preventive Measures

  • Wear a mask: Use a properly fitted mask, especially in crowded or poorly ventilated indoor settings.
  • Maintain social distancing: Keep a safe distance of at least 1 meter from others, even if they don't appear to be sick.
  • Wash hands regularly: Clean your hands frequently with soap and water, or use an alcohol-based hand rub.
  • Get vaccinated: Get vaccinated when it's your turn and follow local guidance on booster doses.
  • Cover your cough: Cover your mouth and nose with your bent elbow or tissue when you cough or sneeze.

Stay home and self-isolate if you feel unwell to avoid spreading the virus to others.
