Topic 2: India in Covid 19

India faced multiple waves of Covid-19. The vaccination drive in India was one of the largest in the world. India reported 45,056,221 confirmed cases and 533,849 deaths during the COVID-19 pandemic, ranking second globally in total cases and third in deaths.

Overview and Timeline

  • First Case: Detected on January 30, 2020, in Kerala, in a student returning from Wuhan, China.
  • Initial Response: A strict nationwide lockdown began on March 25, 2020, to slow transmission and scale up medical infrastructure.
  • The Second Wave: A devastating surge peaked in April–May 2021, driven by new variants and mass gatherings, which caused severe shortages of oxygen and hospital beds.
  • Vaccination Drive: Launched on January 16, 2021, using the Co-WIN digital platform to administer billions of doses nationwide.
  • Impact: While official tolls are substantial, the World Health Organization (WHO) estimates excess mortality to be significantly higher, near 4.7 million.
