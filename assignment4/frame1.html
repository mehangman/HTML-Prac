Topic 1: Covid-19 and its variants

Coronavirus disease (COVID-19) is an infectious disease caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus. Most people infected with the virus experience mild to moderate respiratory illness and recover without requiring special treatment. However, some become seriously ill and require medical attention.

Major Variants of Concern

  • Alpha: First identified in the UK, it was more transmissible than the original strain.
  • Beta: First identified in South Africa, this variant carried mutations that affected immune response.
  • Delta: First identified in India, it was significantly more contagious and caused a global surge in cases.
  • Omicron: First identified in Southern Africa, it spreads much faster than previous variants but often causes less severe disease.

Viruses constantly change through mutation, leading to the emergence of these variants.
